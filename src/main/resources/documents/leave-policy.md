# Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least five working days before the start date. Managers must approve or reject requests within three working days.

Employees may carry forward up to five unused leave days into the next calendar year. Carry-forward days expire on 31 March and cannot be exchanged for cash unless required by local law. Sick leave of up to ten days per year requires a medical certificate if the absence exceeds three consecutive working days.

Unplanned leave on more than three working days in a month must be reported to the employee's manager before 9:00 AM on the first day of absence.

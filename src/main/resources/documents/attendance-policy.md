# Attendance Policy

Full-time employees must work 40 hours per week, normally between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM with a one-hour lunch break. Employees should record arrival and departure using the office access system or the approved time-tracking application.

Employees may work remotely according to the Work from Home Policy, but must be reachable during core collaboration hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. More than three unapproved late arrivals in a rolling 30-day period will be reviewed by the employee's manager.

Employees must not share access badges or allow another person to use their attendance credentials. Attendance records are reviewed monthly for payroll and compliance purposes.

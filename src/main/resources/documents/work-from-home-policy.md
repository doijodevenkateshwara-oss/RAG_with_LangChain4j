# Work from Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to three days per week after completing their probation period. The employee's manager must approve the weekly schedule in advance. Working from home does not change an employee's working hours or performance expectations.

The home workspace must have reliable internet access, a secure connection, and a dedicated area suitable for confidential work. Employees must use company-provided security software, lock their devices when away, and report lost or stolen equipment immediately.

Employees must attend scheduled video meetings and respond to work messages during core hours. The company does not pay a regular monthly internet allowance. Managers may ask an employee to attend the office for team meetings, training, or client work with reasonable notice.

# Employee Benefits

Full-time employees receive medical insurance covering the employee and two dependents. The company pays 80 percent of the premium and employees pay the remaining 20 percent through payroll deduction. Coverage becomes effective on the first day of the month after joining.

Eligible employees receive a retirement contribution of 8 percent of eligible salary, subject to statutory limits. The company also provides 15 days of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 10 days for the secondary caregiver.

The company offers an annual learning allowance of USD 500 for approved courses and certifications. Benefits are managed by the People Operations team and may change after notice required by applicable law.
